Chronicles of Phusza

Realm Lore & Delver's Handbook
💠 The Shattered Seal of Phusza Beneath the moss-choked arches of the forsaken crypt pulses a living shard of Phusza crystal. Warlords, scholars, and treasure seekers descend its torchlit halls seeking ancient relics and the fabled Crown.
⚒️ Forge & D20 Fate Customize your hero across 6 ancestries (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Goblin, Catfolk) and wield Warrior steel or Mage flame. Every portcullis, sarcophagus, leyline monolith, and trap resolves with a live D20 + Stat Modifier check.
🖱️ Controls & Shortcuts • Click anywhere on the dungeon stone floor to walk
• Click glowing [⯁] encounter beacons to inspect & roll D20 checks
• Drag in Character Forge to inspect your 3D hero on the rune pedestal
• Press M anytime to toggle the orchestral music
